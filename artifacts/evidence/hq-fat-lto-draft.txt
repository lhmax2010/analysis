Thank you for the careful review. Our earlier validation did not sufficiently cover native consumption of fat objects. Following your comments, we analyzed this path and added paired LTO/native tests.

Of the 56 synthetic test fixtures in the Tizen ARM matrix, 38 linked and passed runtime checks; the remaining 18 were rejected at link time for incompatible native relocations. All 90 runtime invocations passed. For both GCC and Clang, the tested non-PIC fat inputs passed through their matched LTO paths, while native consumption of the same inputs for shared-library/PIE probes was rejected. The tested failures therefore stopped the build. This conclusion applies to the verified cases and linker settings, including -z text; it is not a guarantee for every native input or configuration.
